# QuinceJam research brief: FigJam feature inventory, competitor additions, tech landscape, open questions

Compiled 2026-10-31 from the research inventory (10 areas), 8 gap-fill passes, critic corrections and 8 tech-landscape topics. Target: a FigJam replacement at quincejam.com on Cloudflare, Turborepo (TS + Go + Rust), free-with-limits now, billing guts ready for later, new DB tech welcome.

How to read this document:

- Part 1 is the complete FigJam checklist, one table per area. "Free/Paid" is FigJam's plan gating today (Starter = free). "Build implication" is what QuinceJam needs in its model/runtime to match. Rows marked **GAP** are things FigJam does not have (confirmed absences) and are candidates for Part 2.
- Part 2 ranks features worth adding on top of FigJam parity.
- Part 3 is the tech landscape with findings tables and a recommendation per topic. Source URLs are kept.
- Part 4 is the decision list.
- Research caveat: the shared web-search budget ran out in every research pass; most facts come from direct fetches of help.figma.com, developers.figma.com, figma.com/release-notes, vendor docs and pricing pages. Items flagged "unverified" could not be confirmed from a primary source.

Critic corrections already applied: FigJam shortcuts are sourced from FigJam articles and the in-app panel (article 360040328653 is Design-only); W (washi), M, Shift+M and Shift+Delete/Backspace (eraser) are official; Chrome extension capture is available on Starter (free); code blocks are free; FigJam pages are paid-only (Starter boards are single-page); Figma Make embeds work on all plans; no dark mode / no canvas background color is a confirmed gap; per-tab memory limit is ~2 GB; marker/highlighter have no pressure, smoothing option or shape recognition (confirmed absences).
